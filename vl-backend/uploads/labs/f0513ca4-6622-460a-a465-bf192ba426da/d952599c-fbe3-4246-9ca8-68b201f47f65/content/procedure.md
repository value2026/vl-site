### Procedure

### Performing the simulation:
#### Switch
- **Turn On/Off**: Used to power On/Off the Van De Graaff Generator.
- **Dissect/Cover**: This button used to see the inside of the Van De Graaff Generator.

#### Slider
- **Change Length**: This is used to change the length of two sphere.
- Power On the Van De Graaff Generator by using switch Turn On. Change the length and observe the discharging time for a particular distance.

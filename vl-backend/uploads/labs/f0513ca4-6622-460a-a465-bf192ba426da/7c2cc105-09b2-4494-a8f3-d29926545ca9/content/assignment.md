## Assignment


<ol>
  <li>
    Verify the <strong>Tangent Law</strong> in magnetism using the <strong>simulator</strong>.
  </li>

  <li>
    Find the <strong>reduction factor</strong> of the Tangent Galvanometer (T.G) when a current of 
    <strong>0.172 A</strong> flows through a circular coil with <strong>20 turns</strong>.
  </li>

  <li>
    Determine the <strong>reduction factor</strong> of the given T.G with <strong>10 turns</strong> and 
    a current of <strong>1 A</strong> flowing through it.
  </li>

  <li>
    Determine the <strong>horizontal intensity</strong> of Earth’s magnetic field if the current flowing 
    through the circular coil is <strong>0.138 A</strong> and the number of turns is <strong>35</strong>.
  </li>

  <li>
    Determine both the <strong>reduction factor of T.G</strong> and the <strong>horizontal intensity</strong> 
    of Earth’s magnetic field, when a current of <strong>0.096 A</strong> flows through a circular coil having 
    <strong>25 turns</strong> and a <strong>radius of 6 cm</strong>.
  </li>
</ol>

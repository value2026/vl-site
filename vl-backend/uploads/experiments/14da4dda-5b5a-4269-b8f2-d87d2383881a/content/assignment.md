## Assignment


<ol>
  <li>
    A solution of urea in water has a boiling point of <strong>100.130 °C</strong>. 
    Calculate the freezing point of the same solution. 
    <br>
    Given: <em>K<sub>f</sub></em> = -1.858 °C, <em>K<sub>b</sub></em> = 0.512 °C.
  </li>
  
  <li>
    A certain solution of benzoic acid in benzene freezes at <strong>3.1 °C</strong>. 
    What information about the number of particles and the structure of benzoic acid at the temperature can be deduced? 
    <br>
    The freezing point of pure benzene is <strong>5.5 °C</strong>, 
    and the cryoscopic constant <em>K<sub>f</sub></em> = -4.90 K·kg·mol<sup>-1</sup>.
  </li>
  
  <li>
    Determine the molecular weight of non-volatile solute (Camphor) by the cryoscopic method using Carbon tetrachloride as the solvent.  
    <br>
    Given: <em>K<sub>f</sub></em> = -29.8 °C·kg·mol<sup>-1</sup>, i = 1, W<sub>A</sub> = 15 g, W<sub>B</sub> = 5 g.
  </li>
  
  <li>
    Determine the freezing point depression constant <em>K<sub>f</sub></em> of the solvent Carbon disulfide using Sulfur as the solute.  
    <br>
    Given: W<sub>A</sub> = 10 g, W<sub>B</sub> = 2 g, M<sub>B</sub> of Sulfur = 256.52 g/mol.
  </li>
  
  <li>
    A solution of sucrose (molecular weight = 342) is prepared by dissolving <strong>34.2 g</strong> of it in <strong>1000 g</strong> of water.  
    Sucrose has a van ’t Hoff factor i = 1.  
    Find the freezing point of the solution in Kelvin.  
    <br>
    Given: <em>K<sub>f</sub></em> for water = -1.86 K·kg·mol<sup>-1</sup>.
  </li>
